# Business Travel Policy

Effective: 1 March 2026. Owner: Finance, Nimbus Corp. Applies to all offices.

## 1. Before you travel

All business trips must be booked through the company travel portal, Nimbus Trips.
Trips with a total estimated cost above the limits below need **pre-approval from your manager** before booking:

| Office | Pre-approval needed above |
|---|---|
| India (Bengaluru) | ₹50,000 per trip |
| Germany (Berlin) | €800 per trip |

Trips outside your home country always need pre-approval, whatever the cost.

## 2. Flights

- Flights with a scheduled duration **under 6 hours**: economy class.
- Flights of **6 hours or more**: premium economy class is allowed.
- Business class is never reimbursed unless approved in writing by a VP.

Book at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## 3. Hotels

| Office | Maximum per night |
|---|---|
| India (Bengaluru) | ₹7,000 |
| Germany (Berlin) | €150 |

The cap applies to the room rate, excluding taxes. In very expensive cities (for example London, New York, Zurich) the cap may be exceeded by up to 50% with manager approval.

## 4. Daily allowance (per diem)

The per diem covers meals and local transport on travel days.

| Office | Per diem |
|---|---|
| India (Bengaluru) | ₹2,500 per day |
| Germany (Berlin) | €45 per day |

No receipts are needed for the per diem.

## 5. Expenses

Submit all travel expenses in Spendly within **30 days** of returning, with receipts for anything above ₹500 or €10.
Late claims may be rejected.
