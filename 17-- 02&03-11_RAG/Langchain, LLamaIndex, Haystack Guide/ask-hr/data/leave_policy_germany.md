# Leave Policy — Germany (Berlin office)

Effective: 1 January 2026. Owner: People Team, Nimbus Corp.
Applies to: all permanent employees based in the Berlin office.

## 1. Annual leave

Employees in Germany receive **28 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, based on a five-day week.
This is above the statutory minimum of 20 days under the Bundesurlaubsgesetz.
Leave requests of up to 3 days need approval from your manager at least 3 working days in advance.
Requests longer than 3 days need at least 2 weeks' notice.

During the 6-month probation period, employees may take at most 5 days of annual leave in total (see the Employee Handbook, section 4).

## 2. Carry-forward of unused leave

Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
Carried-forward days must be used by **31 March** of the following year; any days left after that date lapse.
Statutory leave that could not be taken because of illness is handled according to German law.

## 3. Sick leave

Sick pay follows German law (Entgeltfortzahlungsgesetz): full pay for up to 6 weeks per illness.
Tell your manager on the first day of illness. A doctor's certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) is required from the **4th calendar day** of illness, i.e. for absences longer than 3 days.

## 4. Parental leave

Parental leave (Elternzeit) follows German law: up to 3 years per child, with the state parental allowance (Elterngeld).
In addition, Nimbus Corp pays **4 weeks of full salary** to each parent who takes Elternzeit within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.
Maternity protection (Mutterschutz) of 6 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth applies in full.

## 5. Public holidays

The Berlin office observes all public holidays of the state of Berlin.
