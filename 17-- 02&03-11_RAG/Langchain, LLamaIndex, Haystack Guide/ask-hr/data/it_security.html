IT Security Basics

Last reviewed: February 2026. Owner: IT Security Team. Applies to all employees in all offices.

Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use a passphrase of several random words rather than a short complex word. Never reuse your Nimbus password on any other website. Store passwords only in the company password manager, Nimbus Vault.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA is required on every company account, including email, Slack, the People portal and GitHub. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are not allowed.

Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments. Use the Report button in Outlook, or forward the email to security@nimbus.example. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, contact the IT helpdesk immediately.

Lost or stolen devices

If a company device (laptop, phone or security key) is lost or stolen, notify the IT helpdesk within one hour by calling extension 4357 (4HELP) or +91 80 4000 4357 / +49 30 4000 4357 from outside the office. IT will lock and remotely wipe the device. Then file a police report and send the reference number to IT.

Public Wi-Fi and VPN

On any network outside a Nimbus office (cafés, airports, hotels, home), connect to the company VPN before accessing internal systems.

Software

Install software only from the Nimbus Self Service app. Requests for other software go through the IT portal.
