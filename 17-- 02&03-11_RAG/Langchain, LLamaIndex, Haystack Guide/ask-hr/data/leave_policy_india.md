# Leave Policy — India (Bengaluru office)

Effective: 1 January 2026. Owner: People Team, Nimbus Corp.
Applies to: all permanent employees based in the Bengaluru office.

## 1. Annual leave

Employees in India receive **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at 1.75 days per month, starting from the date of joining.
Leave requests of up to 3 days need approval from your manager at least 3 working days in advance.
Requests longer than 3 days need at least 2 weeks' notice.

During the 6-month probation period, employees may take at most 5 days of annual leave in total (see the Employee Handbook, section 4).

## 2. Carry-forward of unused leave

Up to **10 days** of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
Carried-forward days must be used by **31 March** of the following year; any days left after that date lapse.
Unused leave above the 10-day limit lapses on 31 December and is not paid out, except at the end of employment.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive **12 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year.
For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.
Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## 4. Parental leave

- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of fully paid leave, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.
- **Paternity leave:** 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Adoption leave:** 12 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver.

Notify the People Team at least 8 weeks before the expected start of parental leave.

## 5. Public holidays

The Bengaluru office observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published on the People portal every December.
