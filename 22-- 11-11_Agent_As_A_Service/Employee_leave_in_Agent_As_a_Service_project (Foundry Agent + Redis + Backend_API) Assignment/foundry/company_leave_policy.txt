CONTOSO LTD.
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Fictional policy for training purposes only.

====================================================================
1. PURPOSE
====================================================================
This policy explains the types of leave available to Contoso employees,
how many days each type provides, who is eligible, and how to apply.

====================================================================
2. LEAVE YEAR
====================================================================
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

====================================================================
3. LEAVE TYPES AND ENTITLEMENT
====================================================================
Leave Type       Days per year   Purpose
---------------  --------------  ------------------------------------
Casual Leave     12              Short personal needs and errands
Sick Leave       10              When the employee is unwell
Annual Leave     18              Planned holidays and longer breaks

An employee cannot take more days of a leave type than they are entitled
to in the current leave year.

====================================================================
4. ELIGIBILITY RULES
====================================================================
A leave request is valid only when all the rules that apply are met:

4.1 Entitlement
    The days requested must be within the employee's yearly entitlement
    for that leave type.

4.2 Advance notice
    Casual Leave and Annual Leave must be applied for at least 2 calendar
    days before the first day of leave.
    Example: for leave starting on Friday, apply on Wednesday or earlier.

4.3 Sick Leave exception
    Sick Leave is exempt from the advance-notice rule. It may be applied
    for at short notice, including on the day of absence.

4.4 Manager approval
    All leave requests, including Sick Leave, require approval from the
    employee's manager. Leave is confirmed only after approval.

====================================================================
5. HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
====================================================================
1. Log in to the Contoso HR Portal.
2. Open "Apply for Leave".
3. Choose the leave type, the start and end dates, and add a short reason.
4. Submit the request (at least 2 calendar days in advance, except Sick
   Leave).
5. The request goes to your manager, who approves or rejects it.
6. You receive a notification with the decision in the HR Portal.

For Sick Leave, also inform your manager as early as possible on the day
of absence. Medical details are not required to apply.

====================================================================
6. CHECKING YOUR LEAVE BALANCE
====================================================================
Employees can see how many days they have left for each leave type in the
Contoso HR Portal.

====================================================================
7. MATTERS NOT COVERED
====================================================================
Topics not described in this policy (for example carry-forward of unused
leave, leave encashment, half-day leave, parental leave, or public
holidays) should be raised with the HR team.
